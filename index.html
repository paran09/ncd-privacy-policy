Privacy Policy

Beneficiary screening and health programme records

App: NCD · Android package: com.thurinjapuram.ncd
Effective date: 24 November 2026

This policy explains how NCD handles information about authorized staff and beneficiaries whose screening records staff enter into the app. Privacy enquiries can be directed to the contact listed below.

Information handled by NCD

  • Beneficiary details: name, age, sex, village, address, telephone number when provided, beneficiary identifiers, assigned health facility and screening dates.
  • Health and screening records: blood pressure, blood glucose, screening type and history, suspect and referral status, programme registration status, and notes entered by staff.
  • Staff and account information: account identifier, email where configured, role, health-facility assignment, login credentials, session information and account administration history.
  • Record history: editor identifiers, timestamps, revisions and correction explanations used to maintain accountability.
  • Device storage: display preferences, encrypted session credentials, cached responses and screening submissions queued when connectivity is unavailable.

NCD does not request access to contacts, SMS, camera, microphone or precise device location. Addresses and villages entered by staff are still location-related personal information.

How information is used

NCD uses these records to authenticate staff, restrict access by role and assigned facility, register and find beneficiaries, record screenings and follow-up, track referrals and programme activity, produce reports and maintain correction history. Locally queued screenings can be sent to the service when connectivity is restored.

Who can access information

The service provides access to authorized staff and administrators according to their assigned roles. Data is transmitted to the NCD backend to operate the service.

Data is not shared with outside recipients for their own purposes. Authorized health staff access records through the NCD service.

The backend uses cloud hosting and database infrastructure to store and process records as necessary to operate NCD. These service providers process information as part of providing the service. The public policy page is hosted separately on GitHub Pages; patient records are not published in the policy repository.

Storage and security

NCD uses HTTPS for backend requests, encrypts locally cached clinical data and session credentials with Android security facilities, and disables standard Android app backup and device transfer for app data. Server access is controlled through staff authentication and authorization.

No security measure can guarantee complete protection. Staff should protect their device and login credentials and report suspected unauthorized access using the contact below.

Retention, correction and deletion

Patient screening records are retained indefinitely. There is no scheduled automatic deletion of these records.

To request deletion of patient records, email bloomtvm369@gmail.com with the subject “NCD patient data deletion request”. We delete patient records on request. We may ask for sufficient information to locate the record and verify that the request comes from the patient or an authorized representative. Start with an enquiry; do not send full health records or identity documents in the initial email.

Screening corrections and account changes can have associated audit history. Removing an account or uninstalling the app must not be assumed to erase centrally stored screening records.

For access, correction, staff-account or other privacy requests, contact the same email address. Clinical records and their correction history are separate from a staff login account. Deletion requests should identify which records or account they concern. We will explain any additional verification needed and confirm when the request has been handled.

Intended users

The app's staff login and facility-based workflows are designed for authorized health personnel. Beneficiary records are entered by authorized staff. Individuals whose records are entered do not need to create a patient account to contact us about their information. A parent, guardian or other authorized representative may contact us on behalf of a beneficiary where appropriate.

About this webpage

Patient information must not be submitted through GitHub issues or comments.
